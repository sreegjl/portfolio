practice: impulse & momentum

momentum, impulse, conservation & center of mass

Key equations

Momentum\(\vec{p} = m\vec{v}\)
Newton's 2nd law\(\vec{F}_{net} = \Delta\vec{p}/\Delta t\)
Impulse\(\vec{J} = \vec{F}\,\Delta t\)
Impulse-momentum\(\vec{J}_{net} = \Delta\vec{p}\)
F-t graph\(J = \text{area under curve}\)
Conservation\(\sum \vec{p}_i = \sum \vec{p}_f\)
CM position\(x_{cm} = \sum m_i x_i / \sum m_i\)
CM velocity\(\vec{v}_{cm} = \vec{p}_{tot}/M\)
